1.0.3
- Fixed: a sapient mechanoid was refused any building whose construction requirement sat above the fixed skill level of the mechanoid it was built from, however high its own skill had actually grown - a War Queen with Construction 18 being turned away from something needing 12. Sowing was affected the same way, so high-requirement crops like devilstrand were refused too. Sapient mechanoids are now judged on the skills they actually have.
- Fixed: a gravship launch was cancelled the instant it began if a sapient mechanoid was crewing it, with the ritual window refusing to even close. Before launching, the ship sorts every mechanoid on the map into its cargo manifest - and if a pawn on that manifest is also crewing the launch, the game cancels the ritual, on the reasonable assumption that a mechanoid is freight and could never be crew. A sapient mechanoid is both. They are now left off the manifest when crewing and simply walk aboard like anyone else; ones that aren't crewing still get loaded as before, so nothing is left behind. Requires Odyssey.
- Fixed: when Big and Small failed to determine whether a particular pawn was mechanical, the failure wasn't remembered, so the check was retried - and failed again - on every subsequent call for the rest of the session. A single problem pawn could produce constant errors and noticeable stutter while the log reported it only once. Such a result is now remembered like any other, so it costs a check every few seconds instead of thousands per minute.
